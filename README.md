## ✨ What is MargiQ?

**MargiQ** is a native Android commercial pricing and profitability tool built for Indian pharmacies, retailers, stockists, distributors and people who need to make better purchasing decisions.

It goes beyond a simple markup calculator. MargiQ helps you understand the **actual economic cost of stock**, account for purchase and selling GST, evaluate eligible Input Tax Credit (ITC), include supplier discounts and free-stock schemes, and compare competing products or supplier offers using consistent financial metrics.

> **Know what your stock really costs before deciding what it is worth selling for.**

## 🧾 How the calculations work

MargiQ keeps purchase economics and sales economics distinct. The exact result depends on the selected tax mode, tax classification, ITC eligibility, discount and offer configuration.

### 1. Buy X, Get Y Free

For a qualifying offer, only completed offer cycles generate free stock.

~~~text
Offer cycles = floor(Paid quantity / Buy quantity)

Free packs = Offer cycles × Free quantity

Total received = Paid packs + Free packs

Effective cost per received pack =
    Total economic purchase cost / Total packs received
~~~

**Example — Buy 5, Get 1 Free**

| Calculation | Value |
|:---|---:|
| Paid packs | 5 |
| Free packs | 1 |
| Total received | 6 |
| Cost per paid pack | ₹72.1329 |
| Total economic purchase cost | ₹360.6645 |
| Effective cost per received pack | **₹60.11** |

The free pack is additional inventory, not a percentage discount. Its effect is reflected by allocating the cost of the paid quantity across all units received.

### 2. Supplier discount

A supplier discount reduces the purchase price according to the configured discount percentage. The discounted purchase amount is then used in the existing purchase-tax calculation.

~~~text
Discount amount = Purchase price × Discount rate

Discounted purchase price = Purchase price − Discount amount
~~~

### 3. GST and Input Tax Credit

MargiQ supports independent tax-inclusive and tax-exclusive purchase and selling price modes.

- **Tax-exclusive:** tax is calculated on the taxable purchase or selling base.
- **Tax-inclusive:** the taxable base is derived from the tax-inclusive amount.
- **ITC enabled and eligible:** eligible purchase GST is treated as recoverable credit and excluded from economic purchase cost.
- **ITC disabled or not eligible:** purchase tax remains part of the economic cost under the configured classification.

### 4. Profitability metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| **Net sales revenue** | Selling value excluding applicable output GST when the selling price is tax-inclusive. |
| **Economic purchase cost** | Acquisition cost after configured discounts and eligible ITC treatment. |
| **Profit per unit / pack** | Net sales revenue minus the corresponding effective cost. |
| **Profit margin** | Profit ÷ net sales revenue × 100. |
| **Markup** | Profit ÷ effective purchase cost × 100. |
| **Batch profit** | Projected profit for the calculated sale quantity. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> MargiQ is a commercial pricing and estimation tool, not a tax filing or legal compliance product. Actual GST treatment, ITC admissibility and promotional-scheme taxability depend on applicable law, invoice documentation and professional advice.

## 🔐 Privacy & product principles

MargiQ is designed around a privacy-first, offline-first product philosophy.

- **No advertisements.**
- **No subscriptions or paid feature tiers.**
- **No mandatory registration or phone number.**
- **No mandatory cloud account or cloud sync.**
- **Calculation and history data are stored locally on the device.**
- **No analytics or tracking SDKs are intentionally included.**
- Core calculator and comparison workflows are designed to work offline.
- Internet permission is used for the optional, user-initiated GitHub update check; it is not required for everyday calculations.

Your pricing inputs and saved calculation history are not intended to be uploaded to a MargiQ server.

## 👩‍💻 Build from source

### Requirements

- Android SDK Platform 36 and Build Tools 36.
- JDK 17.
- Git.
- Android Studio or another compatible Kotlin/Gradle development environment.

### Clone

~~~bash
git clone https://github.com/Dr-SuBZeRo/MargiQ.git
cd MargiQ
~~~

### Windows PowerShell

~~~powershell
# Confirm Java
java -version

# Run all local unit tests
.\gradlew.bat test --rerun-tasks

# Run Android lint
.\gradlew.bat lintDebug

# Build debug APK
.\gradlew.bat assembleDebug

# Build signed release APK (requires your own local signing configuration)
.\gradlew.bat assembleRelease

# Build Android App Bundle
.\gradlew.bat bundleRelease
~~~

### Linux / macOS

~~~bash
chmod +x gradlew
./gradlew test
./gradlew lintDebug
./gradlew assembleDebug
./gradlew assembleRelease
./gradlew bundleRelease
~~~

Debug APK output:

~~~text
app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
~~~

Release APK output:

~~~text
app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk
~~~

> Release signing credentials are intentionally not stored in this repository. Configure your own local keystore or environment variables; never commit keystores, passwords or signing properties.
